Our Story

Made slowly,
poured with care.

La Rosée takes its name from the morning dew: the quiet, fresh moment before the day begins. We built a café around that feeling, with carefully sourced coffee, food made in small batches and a room where you are never rushed.

Whether it is your first cup of the day or a long afternoon with friends, we want every visit to feel like a small escape.
